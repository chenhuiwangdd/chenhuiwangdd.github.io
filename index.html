Chenhui Wang / PhD Researcher

I am a PhD candidate in Econometrics and Data Science at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, where I am supervised by Juan Juan Cai and Julia Schaumburg. Before starting my PhD, I studied Mathematics at Tianjin University (BSc & MSc).

My research develops econometric methods for understanding extreme events, focusing on tail risk, dependence among extremes, and heterogeneity in extreme value behaviour across large panels.
