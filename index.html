Chenhui Wang / PhD Researcher

I am a PhD candidate in Econometrics and Data Science at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, where I am supervised by Juan Juan Cai and Julia Schaumburg. My research develops econometric methods for understanding extreme events, focusing on tail risk, dependence among extremes, and heterogeneity in extreme value behaviour across large panels.

My current research studies extreme value indices in large panels, time-varying tail behaviour, and the persistence of extreme temperatures. I am particularly interested in how statistical methods for extremes can help us understand and measure risks arising from financial and climate-related events.

Before starting my PhD, I studied Mathematics at Tianjin University, where I completed both my BSc and MSc.
