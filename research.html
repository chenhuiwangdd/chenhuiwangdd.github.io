Estimation and Inference for the Persistence of Extremely High Temperatures

Econometrics Journal, forthcoming

with Juan Juan Cai, Yicong Lin, and Julia Schaumburg

Proposes a nonparametric framework for estimating the extremal index, verifying a local dependence condition, and selecting its tuning parameter. Establishes asymptotic normality and consistency of the diagnostic and selection procedures, and documents persistent extreme heat in nine European cities.

Clustering Extreme Value Indices in Large Panels

R&R (round 2), Journal of the American Statistical Association

with Juan Juan Cai, Yicong Lin, and Julia Schaumburg

Develops a clustering framework for heterogeneous extreme value indices in large panels. Establishes consistency of the estimated group structure and asymptotic inference for group-wise tail index estimators, enabling data-driven identification of heterogeneity in extreme tail risk.
